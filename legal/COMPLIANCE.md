# Academic Ethics, Dual-Use Technology & Legal Compliance

## 1. Research Scope & Academic Freedom
This project constitutes architectural research into network transport resilience, cryptography, and traffic analysis countermeasures. All specifications, reference implementations, and benchmark configurations are provided strictly for:
- Academic study and cybersecurity education.
- Defending communications integrity and availability in adversarial networks.
- Verification of network interference measurement methodologies (OONI / Citizen Lab alignment).

---

## 2. Dual-Use Technology Framework
Transport layer obfuscation and encryption technologies possess inherent dual-use characteristics. While these tools can circumvent network-level access controls, their primary engineering purpose is:
1. **Preserving Confidentiality**: Preventing deep packet inspection systems from cataloging private user interactions.
2. **Mitigating Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) Attacks**: Thwarting content tampering, DNS hijacking, and traffic injection by intermediate network operators.
3. **Enhancing Transport Reliability**: Sustaining throughput in impaired, congested, or artificially throttled networks.

---

## 3. Compliance & Responsible Deployment
Users and systems engineers deploying configurations from this repository must:
- Ensure compliance with applicable national, state, and international telecommunications regulations.
- Refrain from using these configurations for unauthorized penetration testing or unauthorized access to computer systems (CFAA / EU NIS2 / Budapest Convention compliance).
- Maintain dedicated operational infrastructure isolated from critical production environments when evaluating defensive steganography tools.
